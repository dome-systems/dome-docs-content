**Connect** attaches callers and AI resources to the runtime surface you built in Dome. Configure tools, models, and pools; Dome stores their upstream credentials, so agents authenticate to Dome rather than to every backend.

## What you connect

<Cards>
  <Card title="Callers" href="/connect/callers">
    Configure trusted callers and end-user verification when access depends on the person an agent represents.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Tools" href="/connect/tools">
    Attach MCP servers and related backends as resources a gateway can expose.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Models" href="/connect/models">
    Connect LLM providers and hold their credentials server-side.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Pools" href="/connect/pools">
    Group model connections behind one name with routing and failover.
  </Card>
</Cards>

An agent reaches these resources only through a [gateway](/build/gateways) that admits it, and never holds their upstream credentials. [rules](/reference/glossary#rule), [guards](/reference/glossary#guard), and [quotas](/reference/glossary#quota) still decide what the call may do, what content may pass, and how much spend is allowed after admission.

Choose between standing and delegated identity on [Identity Patterns](/concepts/identity-patterns). Refer to [Build](/build) for agents, gateways, and triggers, or [Architecture](/concepts/architecture) for the full request path.

## Typical workflow

1. Add [tools](/connect/tools), [models](/connect/models), and [pools](/connect/pools) when you need a stable name, routing, or failover.
2. Add those resources to a [gateway](/build/gateways) you created in [Build](/build).
3. Configure [callers](/connect/callers) or [delegated agents](/build/agents/delegated) when access needs verified end-user identity.
4. Continue to [Govern](/govern) to define what the agent may do after the gateway admits it.
