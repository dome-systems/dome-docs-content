**Operate** makes governed traffic visible and actionable. Query the durable audit trail, stream live events, configure integrations, and subscribe external systems to signed deliveries.

## What you operate

<Cards>
  <Card title="Audit events" href="/operate/audit">
    Query and filter the durable Audit v1 trail by class, actor, resource, correlation, and payload, then follow activity chains.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Stream live events" href="/operate/observe">
    Watch that trail in real time alongside calls and denials, health and latency, token usage and cost, and quotas.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Integrations" href="/operate/integrations">
    Configure audit destinations and enterprise single sign-on for the systems that work alongside Dome.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Subscribe to events" href="/operate/webhooks">
    Push a public projection of selected events to your HTTPS endpoint or a provider, signed, retried, and replayable.
  </Card>
</Cards>

Audit events is the durable record. Stream watches activity inside Dome; subscribe delivers selected public events when another system must react. [Integrations](/operate/integrations) configures supported destinations for retained audit evidence. Neither replaces batch export under [Manage](/manage/export). The [Callers](/connect/callers#inspect-callers) registry under Connect is a separate projection derived from the same evidence.

See [Audit events](/concepts/audit) for how the one trail works, the [Event catalog](/reference/events/catalog) for every event type, and [Event contracts](/reference/events/contracts) for the shared envelope.

## Typical workflow

1. [Audit events](/operate/audit) to query, get, and correlate retained events when you investigate a decision or prepare evidence.
2. [Stream live events](/operate/observe) for governed traffic in real time, including token usage, cost, OpenTelemetry, and product telemetry.
3. [Integrate systems](/operate/integrations) to configure audit destinations or enterprise single sign-on.
4. [Subscribe to events](/operate/webhooks) to create destinations and subscriptions for the event types your automation must handle.

Afterward you can add destinations, widen subscriptions, or change retention without touching Connect or Govern configuration.
