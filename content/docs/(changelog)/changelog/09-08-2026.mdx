## Self-serve plans and billing

- Choose and purchase a self-serve Pro or Team plan from Dashboard.
- Add a billing contact and payment method as part of a purchase, then manage saved payment methods from Billing.
- Review the price before confirming a plan or capacity change, including the amount due today and the next recurring charge.
- Manage purchased workspace or user capacity from Billing. Dome warns you when you are paying for unused workspace capacity.
- Compare plans with clearer limits and billing terminology, including organization-wide user limits.
